The claim map

Every broad claim in the library placed twice over: left to right by when its evidence landed, and top to bottom by how general it is. A mark's area is the number of curated papers attached to it, and red marks a claim somebody argues with. The three other prototypes draw the graph's edges; this one is the page that fits on one screen.
